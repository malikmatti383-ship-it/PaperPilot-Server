Privacy Policy — PaperPilot

Last updated: 21 October 2026

PaperPilot is a business app for paper shops to manage stock, sales, credit and reports. This policy explains what data we collect and how we use it.

What we collect

  • Account info: when you sign in with Gmail, we receive your name, email address and basic profile information from Google. We ask only for the basic sign-in details (name, email, profile).
  • Your business records: the stock, sales, credit and report entries you create in the app. They are stored on your device first, and when the internet is available they are automatically backed up to our secure server under your Gmail account.
  • Subscription info: your plan, payment reference (the transaction ID you submit), and subscription expiry date.

How we use it

  • To keep your records backed up and in sync across your devices.
  • To manage your subscription (activation, expiry, renewal).
  • To contact you about your subscription or important app changes.

What we do NOT do

  • We do not sell your data or show advertising in the app.
  • We do not share your records with any third party. Your data goes only to your device and our server — we do not currently store it in your Google Drive.
  • We do not process card or bank payments inside the app: you pay directly to the owner's payment accounts shown in the app, and only the transaction reference you submit is stored.

Your choices

  • You can log out of the app at any time from Settings; logging out ends your session on that device. Your previously synced records stay on the server unless you ask us to delete them.
  • To request a copy or deletion of your account data, email us at the address below.
